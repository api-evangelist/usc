A donation from Stevie Nicks establishes the Stevie Nicks and Joseph Sugerman, MD, Endowed Chair in Otolaryngology at USC. The gift deepens Nicks' longstanding connection to the university and supports research and clinical care in ear, nose, and throat medicine.
