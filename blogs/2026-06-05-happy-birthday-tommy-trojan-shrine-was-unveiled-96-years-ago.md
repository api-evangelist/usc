The Tommy Trojan statue icon, modeled after a number of USC football players, represents the five Trojan qualities: faithful, scholarly, skillful, courageous, and ambitious. The statue was unveiled on June 6, 1930 and remains a central symbol of USC identity on the University Park campus.
